### AI Governance Agent — SDD System

An intelligent governance platform designed to analyze operational data, automate decision-making, and support Spec-Driven Development workflows.

The system helps reduce manual oversight, improve consistency, and enforce business rules at scale using AI-assisted reasoning, structured specifications, and automated validation pipelines.

**Focus areas:** AI agents, automation, governance, decision systems, data analysis, compliance workflows.

---

### High-Performance CRM Platform

A proprietary CRM platform built for real-time data processing and high-volume customer interaction management.

The architecture was designed to support fast response times, scalable workflows, structured customer data, and business-critical automation across core CRM operations.

**Focus areas:** scalable architecture, CRM, APIs, real-time workflows, performance optimization.

---

### iFood Clone — Multi-System Delivery Platform

A complete food delivery ecosystem composed of four integrated applications:

- Customer-facing digital menu
- Restaurant admin dashboard
- Delivery driver tracking and order collection system
- Master admin panel for subscriptions and restaurant management

The system was designed to simulate a real-world marketplace architecture with separated responsibilities, user roles, and operational workflows.

**Focus areas:** full-stack development, dashboards, multi-role systems, delivery workflow, SaaS architecture.

---

### Lead Generation Platform — Brazilian Business Database

A B2B lead generation and data enrichment platform built on top of a large Brazilian business database with over 60 million registered companies.

The platform provides advanced filtering, segmentation, and search capabilities to help businesses identify qualified prospects based on location, industry, company profile, and strategic criteria.

**Focus areas:** data engineering, scraping, enrichment, filters, search systems, B2B SaaS.
